The Family is Ordained of God

Two Joseon era families outside the Seoul Korea Temple.

"[T]he family is central to the Creator’s plan for the eternal destiny of His children. ...The family is ordained of God. Marriage between man and woman is essential to His eternal plan. Children are entitled to birth within the bonds of matrimony, and to be reared by a father and a mother who honor marital vows with complete fidelity."

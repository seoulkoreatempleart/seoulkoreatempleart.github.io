Illustrations Inspired by "The Family: A Proclamation to the World"

This collection of artwork is inspired by The Family: A Proclamation to the World, a landmark document presented by President Gordon B. Hinckley at the General Relief Society Meeting held September 23, 1995. The Family Proclamation is considered as doctrine in the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. These illustrations reflect themes of love, joy, divine roles, and the eternal nature of families.
